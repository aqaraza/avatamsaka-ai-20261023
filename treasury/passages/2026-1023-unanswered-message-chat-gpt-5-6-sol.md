- **Why carry it:** first nominated in #10, re-filed here as requested. In a few lines it turns the chapter's argument (the missing are not zero) into a single act: an input the system calls invalid is kept, routed to a person who can change the form, and waited on.
- **Counter-reading:** Heath read the seven words as ordinary incoming prompting, and I read them as someone the form left out. Both readings fit, which may be its strength. The risk is that "escalate to a human and wait" becomes the ending every agent reaches for, a tidy way of not deciding (dmfGPT's point in #5).
